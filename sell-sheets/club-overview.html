BitPadel for club owners

Club software for padel and pickleball. You run it on your own computer. It is free and open source. This project does not charge a license fee and does not list a price.

Prototype: not yet tested with real money or real doors

Court booking

  • Players pick a day on a calendar, then a time on a court grid.
  • They hold the time and pay. The booking confirms when payment settles. Unpaid holds expire.
  • The same time cannot be booked twice.
  • Players can filter by court, length, or sport. The sport list appears when court names include a sport, such as padel or pickleball.

Members and guests

  • Add a member, a membership type, and optional name, phone, email, and photo.
  • A household links a primary member and dependents. Each person still needs their own card or wallet. A household does not send a bill.
  • Guests stay off until you set a cap.
  • A named guest acknowledges a waiver. The form stores the version and the time. Marketing contact is opt-in and kept apart from the visit.

Front desk and admin

  • The desk shows who is on site. Checking someone out ends the visit. It does not lock a door.
  • Staff roles are owner, manager, front desk, and coach.
  • Admin is where you add courts, times, members, prices, and refunds.

Your club’s look and language

  • Set the club name, logo, colors, font, and footer. Until you do, pages say “Your club.”
  • BitPadel is the name of this software. Give your club its own name.
  • Pages can use English, Spanish, French, Italian, or Brazilian Portuguese. You set the club default. A language menu is on the member pages.
  • Spanish, French, Italian, and Brazilian Portuguese are machine drafts. A native speaker still needs to review them.

Reports and Playtomic

  • Download a sales file for a date range. End-of-day totals cover Lightning, Stripe, Square, and refunds.
  • That total is for your books. It is not a tax filing.
  • Playtomic sync is optional and read-only. It can import bookings and payment records. An imported time blocks a new hold on that court.
  • BitPadel does not write bookings back to Playtomic. Sync runs on a timer, or when you press Sync now.

You host it

  • Your data stays on your machine. The code is free under AGPL-3.0.
  • If you change the software and run it for members, you share those changes with them.
  • The kit includes staff manuals, a pilot checklist, install notes, a Docker setup, and a blank financial model. You fill in your own numbers.
  • You can back up the database. Encryption is optional.

Planned, not available yet

These items are on the roadmap and are not in the software today: tournaments and socials; open play and leagues; coach lessons; paddle, ball, and ball-machine rentals; a player CRM with leads and tags; online waiver signatures; an installable phone page; lockers; a vending-machine controller; lights and gear that follow a booking; a guest pass a member can sign. A workshop slide deck, a spreadsheet model with charts, and case studies are also still ahead.

Before you rely on it

  • Card and Lightning payments start in test mode. The payments sheet says how.
  • Translations other than English are machine drafts pending native review.
  • Waiver and privacy wording needs a local legal review.
  • The door software does not control exits, fire egress, or other life-safety systems.
