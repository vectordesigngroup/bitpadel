# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want BitPadel to be welcoming to everyone: players of every level, Bitcoin newcomers and veterans, and people of every background, identity and ability.

## Expected behavior
- Be kind, patient and constructive, on the court and in the repo.
- Assume good intent; explain rather than mock, especially with beginners.
- Respect differing opinions about Bitcoin, sports and business.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or sexualized content.
- Shilling, pumping, scams, or soliciting people for money or investments.
- Sharing anyone's private information without permission.
- Asking anyone for seed phrases, private keys or funds.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove content, close issues or pull requests, and block participants who break this code. Report problems by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers through the repository.
