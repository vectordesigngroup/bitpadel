# Tournaments and leagues

The software plan for running these on club-box (draws, court blocks, Lightning entry fees, signed check-in and scores) is in [`tournaments.md`](tournaments.md).

## Formats that work on one court
| Format | How it runs | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Americano / Mexicano socials** | Rotating partners, short timed games, individual points | Great for beginners and community nights |
| **Ladders** | Players or pairs challenge those above them over weeks | Keeps members booking |
| **Leagues** | Fixed pairs, weekly matches, a season with playoffs | Predictable court use |
| **King/Queen of the court** | Winners stay on, fast rotation | Events and open days |
| **Bracket tournaments** | Seeded pairs, group stage plus knockout | A few times a year; good for sponsors and streaming |

With more courts, add divisions (beginner/intermediate/advanced, mixed, juniors, seniors) and sanctioned events through your national padel association.

## Running a tournament
- Registration (Lightning and card), waiver, skill-level declaration.
- A draw/bracket tool shown on the TV wall.
- Officials or self-refereeing rules published in advance.
- A run-of-show: [`../templates/event-run-of-show.md`](../templates/event-run-of-show.md).
- Streaming or filming: get consent in the registration terms.

## Bitcoin prizes and giveaways
- **Fund the prize before you advertise it**, ideally through a named sponsor.
- If entry fees are involved, confirm with an attorney that the event is a contest of skill under your local rules and not a lottery or illegal gambling.
- Publish official rules: eligibility, how winners are decided, prize amount (fixed sats or a local-currency value, and on what date), tax reporting, disputes.
- Bitcoin prizes are usually taxable to the winner at their value when awarded; your accountant will tell you what reporting you owe.
- Hardware wallets, books and gear make good non-cash prizes.

## Leagues as community
Pair every league night with something social: a meetup, a short Bitcoin talk, or a sponsor's demo. That's the hub and the sport feeding each other.
