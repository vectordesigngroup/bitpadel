# Concept and phases

## The model in one paragraph
A BitPadel club is a padel (and optionally pickleball) venue that is also a **local Bitcoin hub**: it takes Lightning payments for courts, the shop and events; teaches local people and small businesses how Bitcoin works; hosts meetups; and builds a community around both. The sport brings people in. The hub gives them a reason to stay, and gives Bitcoin-friendly businesses a reason to sponsor.

## Revenue lines to consider
Court rentals · memberships · pay-per-play/day passes · lessons and clinics · leagues and tournaments · pro shop (gear, drinks, Bitcoin corner) · event space rental · workshops/conferences (ticketed or sponsor-covered) · sponsorships · streaming/content (later).

Every price is yours to set from local research. The kit gives a blank model in [`../templates/financial-model.csv`](../templates/financial-model.csv).

## Phases

| Phase | What it looks like | Goal | Move on when… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Community first** | No court yet. Waitlist, social accounts, a founding-member interest page, meetups and workshops at partner venues | Prove demand, collect contacts with consent | Your own go/no-go thresholds (waitlist size, founding-member interest, a confirmed site) |
| **1. Pilot court** | One court (permanent, portable or rent-to-own) at a leased lot, warehouse or host club, with a portable clubhouse/shop | Prove people will pay and come back; learn the operations | Occupancy, member retention and community metrics you set in advance |
| **2. Small club** | Several courts, a real clubhouse, lounge, pro shop with Bitcoin corner, member-area access | Sustainable operations and a regular events calendar | Waitlist for court time, events selling out |
| **3. Flagship** | Many padel and pickleball courts, education room/conference space, food and drink, possibly fitness/wellness | Regional destination and Bitcoin hub | — |

## Principles
1. **Start small.** One court and a strong community beats a big build with no members.
2. **Lightning everywhere, cards too.** Make Bitcoin the easy, welcome option, never the only one.
3. **Education, not promotion.** Teach how Bitcoin works; never tell people what to buy (see the [DISCLAIMER](../DISCLAIMER.md)).
4. **Sponsorship, not investment.** Sponsors get visibility and program support, not ownership or returns.
5. **Write down your go/no-go numbers before you start**, so the next phase is a decision, not a hope.
6. **Open by default.** Share what you learn back to this kit.
