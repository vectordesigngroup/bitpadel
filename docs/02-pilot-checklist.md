# Pilot checklist (one court)

Use this as a working list with your site owner, permitting office, insurance broker and attorney. Add local rules; they vary a lot by city, county and country.

## Court
- [ ] Court type: permanent install, portable/panoramic (anchored on a slab), or rent-to-own
- [ ] Standard size and clearances (a regulation padel court is 20 m × 10 m; check the current federation spec for run-off and height)
- [ ] Indoor clear height (check the federation's minimum and recommended ceiling height) or outdoor orientation (north-south reduces sun glare)
- [ ] Surface/slab: flatness tolerance, drainage, anchoring method, who pours it
- [ ] Lighting: lux level for play and streaming, glare control, curfew rules for outdoor lighting
- [ ] Shade and seating for outdoor courts (wind loads for shade structures need engineering)
- [ ] Imports: if the court ships from overseas, get a customs broker's duty/tariff estimate, freight quote and lead time in writing
- [ ] Warranty, installer, spare glass/mesh panels and maintenance schedule

## Site
- [ ] Zoning allows commercial/indoor or outdoor recreation (get it in writing from planning staff)
- [ ] Lease or host-site agreement: term, rent or revenue share, who pays utilities, right to install the court, removal at the end, renewal/expansion options
- [ ] Parking, access, neighbors, noise
- [ ] Restrooms and water (existing, portable, or trailer)
- [ ] Power for lighting, POS, Wi-Fi, cameras and (later) door controllers
- [ ] Internet: reliable connection for payments and booking (have a mobile backup)

## Portable clubhouse / pro shop
- [ ] Modular building, container or trailer: size, delivery, setup, tie-downs
- [ ] Whether it needs its own permit, foundation or ADA ramp
- [ ] Layout: check-in/POS, shop shelves, Bitcoin corner (see the [hub playbook](03-bitcoin-hub-playbook.md)), storage, staff area
- [ ] Shade/porch area for players and events

## Permits and compliance
- [ ] Building/installation permits for the court, slab, lighting, shade and clubhouse
- [ ] Certificate of occupancy / change of use if needed
- [ ] Fire code: occupancy limits for events, exits, extinguishers
- [ ] Accessibility (ADA or local equivalent): accessible route, parking, restroom, check-in
- [ ] Business registration, local business tax receipt, sales tax registration
- [ ] Signage permit
- [ ] Food and alcohol: permits, or a clear "no alcohol" rule until licensed
- [ ] Pre-sales of memberships before opening: some places regulate prepaid memberships (escrow, bonds, cancellation rights). **Ask an attorney before selling anything before opening.**

## Insurance and waivers
- [ ] General liability and participant injury coverage
- [ ] Property/contents (court, clubhouse, shop stock)
- [ ] Workers' compensation for staff where required
- [ ] Event coverage and certificate-of-insurance rules for renters
- [ ] Liability waiver and member terms (parents/guardians sign for minors)
- [ ] Ask the broker about: Bitcoin payments, unstaffed hours, app-based door access, streaming/filming

## Staffing
- [ ] Who opens, closes and handles bookings, payments, refunds and incidents
- [ ] Coach/pro: certification, background check for youth programs
- [ ] Employee vs contractor classification (get it right locally)
- [ ] Staff training: Lightning payments and refunds, scam awareness, first aid/AED, emergency plan
- [ ] Volunteer/partner speakers for the Bitcoin hub

## Payments and booking
- [ ] Booking software chosen (see [Lightning integrations](04-lightning-integrations.md))
- [ ] Card processor plus a Lightning processor, both tested end to end
- [ ] Refund and cancellation policy written and posted
- [ ] Bookkeeping export for every payment method (Lightning payments recorded with their dollar or local-currency value at receipt)

## Before opening day
- [ ] Soft-launch week with founding members
- [ ] Emergency action plan posted (address for 911, AED location, heat policy)
- [ ] Privacy policy and marketing consent on every sign-up form
- [ ] Go/no-go metrics for Phase 2 written down
