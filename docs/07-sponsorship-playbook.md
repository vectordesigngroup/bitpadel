# Sponsorship playbook

**Sponsorship, not investment.** Sponsors pay for visibility and to support the club's programs. They receive no ownership, profit share, revenue share or financial return, and your materials should say so plainly.

## Who sponsors a BitPadel club
- Bitcoin companies: wallets, exchanges/brokerages, payment processors, hardware makers, education platforms, conferences.
- Bitcoin-friendly local businesses: the ones that learned to accept Lightning at your workshops are your best prospects.
- Sports brands: paddles, balls, apparel, court builders.
- Local professionals: CPAs, attorneys, financial planners (subject to their own marketing rules), realtors.

## Package ideas (set your own prices)
| Package | Deliverables |
|---|---|
| **Court naming** | Name on a court, signage, mentions in bookings and streams |
| **Bitcoin Education Partner** | Naming of the workshop series or education station; logo on curriculum slides; a speaking slot (education only) |
| **Conference title sponsor** | Naming of the small-business conference, stage branding, recap video credit |
| **Lightning lab sponsor** | Supplies POS hardware/software and demo sats for hands-on labs |
| **Meetup host** | Food and drinks at meetups, a welcome slide, social mentions |
| **Tournament / prize sponsor** | Bracket naming, prize funding (see [tournament rules](05-tournaments-and-leagues.md)) |
| **Screen partner** | A logo loop on the TV wall (check the market-data license first) |
| **Founding partner** | Bundle of the above for the first season |

## How to sell it
1. Lead with the **community and the hub**: who comes, what they learn, how many local businesses you help adopt Lightning.
2. Show proof: event photos, attendance, waitlist size, social reach.
3. Use [`../templates/sponsor-one-pager.md`](../templates/sponsor-one-pager.md).
4. Put every deal in a short written agreement: deliverables, dates, fee, logo use, cancellation, and a statement that sponsorship grants no ownership or financial return.
5. Report back after each event with photos and numbers. Renewals come from reports.

## Rules of thumb
- Don't name a company as a partner or sponsor in public until it has signed.
- Label sponsored sessions and posts as sponsored.
- Sponsors can teach, not pitch investments, on your stage.
- Keep sponsor money for programs and events separate in your books.
