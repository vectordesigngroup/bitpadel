# Bitcoin hub playbook

The hub is what makes a BitPadel club more than courts: a regular place where people and local businesses learn Bitcoin, use Lightning, and meet each other.

> **Education, not advice.** Hub programs teach how Bitcoin and Lightning work. They never recommend buying, selling or holding anything, predict prices, or promote investment products. Tax and accounting questions go to the attendee's own CPA or tax professional. See the [DISCLAIMER](../DISCLAIMER.md).

## 1. Small-business Bitcoin conferences and workshops

**Goal:** help local small businesses understand Bitcoin and start accepting Lightning payments.

### Formats
| Format | Shape | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Evening workshop | One topic, a short talk plus a hands-on lab | Easiest to run monthly or whenever you have a speaker |
| Small-business conference | Half or full day: talks, a Lightning lab, a panel of local businesses already accepting Bitcoin, padel social play | A few times a year; the anchor for sponsors |
| Office hours | Drop-in help at the education station | Good for follow-up after workshops |

Pick a cadence you can sustain with your volunteers and staff. Capacity depends on your venue's fire-code occupancy.

### Audience
Local owner-operators (restaurants, cafés, retail, trades, services), bookkeepers, entrepreneurship students, chamber of commerce and small-business development center members, and club members who own businesses.

### Curriculum outline
1. **Why Bitcoin.** Money basics, what Bitcoin is and isn't, a short history, volatility explained plainly. No price talk beyond "it moves a lot."
2. **Accepting Lightning.**
   - What Lightning is and why it suits small payments.
   - Point-of-sale options: self-hosted (BTCPay Server), lightweight (LNbits), hosted processors and Lightning-enabled POS apps.
   - Pricing in local currency vs sats; settling to Bitcoin vs converting to local currency.
   - Refunds, tips, receipts.
   - **Lab:** every attendee sends and receives a small payment with a demo wallet.
3. **Custody basics.** Hot vs cold storage, seed phrases, hardware wallets, multisig for businesses, who holds keys in a business, succession, common scams.
4. **Accounting and tax pointers, not advice.** Why each Bitcoin payment is a recordkeeping event, cost basis, sales tax still applies, paying staff or contractors in Bitcoin, accounting rules for businesses holding Bitcoin. **Then: talk to your CPA.** Ideally a local CPA presents this module and gives their own disclaimer.
5. **Security and compliance awareness.** Phishing and social engineering; when a business might fall under money-transmitter or similar rules (for example, holding or sending Bitcoin for other people); "talk to an attorney" pointers.
6. **Case study.** The club itself: how it takes Lightning for bookings and the shop.

### Speaker guidelines (share with every speaker)
- Teach; don't sell. No investment recommendations, price predictions or product pitches.
- Disclose sponsorships and conflicts at the start of the talk.
- No collecting attendees' keys, seeds or funds, ever.
- Slides can be published under the kit's CC BY-SA license if the speaker agrees.

### Run it
Use [`../templates/event-run-of-show.md`](../templates/event-run-of-show.md). Measure: attendees, businesses that finish the Lightning lab, businesses that report accepting Lightning a month later (short follow-up survey), repeat attendance.

### Open curriculum
If you publish your workshop materials under an open license, consider contributing them back here. Open-licensed Bitcoin education may also fit education grant programs such as the OpenSats Education Initiative (check their current criteria).

## 2. Bitcoin meetups
- Co-host with your city's existing Bitcoin meetup if there is one; don't compete with it.
- Format idea: a short talk or open Q&A, then social round-robin play ("sats social").
- Keep it free or cheap, and keep it about people, not prices.
- Rules: no shilling tokens or investment schemes, no soliciting attendees for money.

## 3. Event space rental
Rent the court and lounge for corporate events, birthday parties, team offsites, watch parties and side events for regional Bitcoin conferences.
- A written rental agreement: date/time, headcount, deposit, cancellation, damage, cleaning.
- Certificate of insurance from renters for larger events.
- Occupancy limits from your fire code; alcohol only within your license or the renter's caterer license.
- Accept Lightning and cards for deposits and balances.

## 4. Pro-shop Bitcoin corner
| Piece | What it is | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| **Hardware wallets** | Display and sell signing devices from established makers, as an authorized reseller or via referral | Buy only from the maker or authorized distributors; sell sealed, tamper-evident stock; **staff never set up devices or touch seed phrases**; sales tax; returns policy |
| **Books** | A Bitcoin reading shelf: beginner guides, history, technical primers, kids' books. Local authors are a great fit | Consignment or wholesale terms; sales tax |
| **TV wall** | Live Bitcoin price, charts, mempool/fee data, Lightning network stats, plus court schedule, brackets and event promos | Market-data and charting feeds often restrict **public/commercial display**: check the license. Data only, no "buy now" messaging |
| **Education station** | A table or kiosk with a demo wallet, a "send your first sats" walkthrough, printed guides and the QR code for the next workshop | Demo wallets hold only tiny amounts; no handling of visitors' funds |
| **Lightning at the counter** | Every item and drink payable with Lightning | Same processor as bookings; see [Lightning integrations](04-lightning-integrations.md) |
