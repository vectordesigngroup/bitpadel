# Community growth

Build the community before the court opens. A waitlist with real people on it helps you choose a site, size the pilot and talk to sponsors.

## 1. Waitlist
- A simple form: name, email, ZIP/postcode, interests (padel, pickleball, lessons, events, Bitcoin workshops), how they heard about you, and an explicit **marketing consent** checkbox.
- Store only what you need. Publish a privacy policy. Follow anti-spam rules for email.
- Send a short welcome email and then a regular (but not too frequent) update.

## 2. Founding members
- A founding-member program rewards early believers with benefits (priority booking, clinics, gear, event access).
- Use [`../templates/founding-member-page.html`](../templates/founding-member-page.html). It deliberately has **no prices**.
- **Before you take any money before opening**, ask an attorney about prepaid membership rules where you are (escrow, bonds, cancellation rights). Collect *interest* first; sell later.
- Cap the number so the court isn't oversold. Roughly estimate how many members one court can serve by checking booking capacity in your own model.
- Keep language about **membership and play**. Don't describe founding memberships as an investment, ownership, or anything that pays a return.

## 3. Reddit
- Create a city subreddit for padel (for example r/[City]Padel) and post rules, a pinned FAQ and a link to the waitlist.
- Start "Restricted" (anyone can read, approved users post) while it's small, then switch to Public.
- Be useful: where to play now, beginner tips, gear questions, events. Follow each subreddit's self-promotion rules.

## 4. Instagram (and short video)
- A link in bio that goes to the waitlist or founding page.
- Content: court-build progress, first rallies, renders of the plan, coach tips, meetup recaps, local business spotlights from the Lightning workshops.
- Vertical 9:16 videos with captions; consistent brand colors.
- Never post prices, terms or anything that reads like an investment offer before your attorney has approved it.

## 5. Local partners
- Bitcoin meetup groups, small-business development centers, chambers of commerce, universities and their entrepreneurship groups, racquet-sport clubs, local coaches.
- Offer co-hosted events, not just logo swaps.

## 6. Metrics
| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Waitlist sign-ups (with consent) | Demand |
| Founding-member interest | Willingness to commit |
| Event/meetup attendance | Community strength |
| Businesses onboarded to Lightning | Hub impact |
| Social followers and engagement | Reach for sponsors |

Set your own targets; there are no universal numbers.
