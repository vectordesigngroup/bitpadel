Sell sheet · 3 of 3 · Access

Doors, check-in, and pages members actually use

Members come in with a Lightning wallet or an NFC card. Guests check in at the desk or kiosk. Pages open in a phone browser. The language menu is on those pages.

Prototype: not yet tested with real money or real doors

What the door software does

  • It can send one short entry pulse to a lock you connect.
  • It does not lock a door shut. It does not control exits, fire egress, or other life-safety systems. People leave without this software.
  • If the screen cannot reach the server, use a fob, a PIN, or staff. Those paths are physical. They are not built into this app.
  • A licensed installer and your fire marshal design the door. Notes for that conversation are in the kit. They are not a certification.

Wallet unlock

  • A member enrolls a Lightning wallet once.
  • The kiosk shows a QR code that rotates. The wallet signs it. No sats move.
  • An active member gets an entry pulse. The attempt is written to a log. Expired, repeated, or revoked tries are refused.

NFC cards

  • NTAG 424 DNA cards can be enrolled, revoked, or marked lost. A programming command is in the kit.
  • A DESFire card can be checked when a reader finishes the card session. Programming a DESFire card is not included.
  • One member can hold more than one card and more than one wallet.
  • Copied UID-only cards stay off unless you turn on a development switch.

Kiosk and check-in

  • The kiosk is a browser page on the club network. You pick the area. The safety lines stay on the screen.
  • A successful unlock records a check-in. If you saved a photo, the kiosk can show the name and photo.
  • The front desk lists who is still on site.

Guests

  • After a member is in, staff can add guests up to the cap you set. The cap starts at zero, so guests stay off until you raise it.
  • A named guest fills in a short form and acknowledges a waiver. The form stores the waiver version and the time.
  • Members are not asked to sign. An online signature for everyone is planned, not available.
  • The marketing box starts unchecked. That consent is stored apart from the visit.

On a phone

  • Booking, account, guest, kiosk, and desk pages open in a phone browser.
  • The court grid scrolls sideways when a day has many courts.
  • An installable page that puts booking, the door, and the tab in one home-screen app is planned. It is not available.

Language menu

  • English, Spanish, French, Italian, or Brazilian Portuguese. The choice applies in this browser.
  • After a member signs in, the same choice can be saved on their profile. Receipts use that language when email is set up.
  • Translations other than English are machine drafts pending native review. Waiver, consent, and door-safety lines need a local review before you rely on them.

Hours

You can limit an area to set hours. You can also allow entry only around a member’s booked court time. That booking rule is off until you turn it on. A closed area is not unlocked.

Planned, not available yet

Lockers. A one-day guest pass a member can sign. Court lights, a ball machine, and a gear cabinet that work only during a booking. The installable phone page described above. Online waiver signatures for members and players.
