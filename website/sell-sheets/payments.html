Sell sheet · 2 of 3 · Payments

Players pay in sats or by card

Court time can be paid with Lightning, or with Stripe or Square when you turn those on. You set every amount. BitPadel does not include prices.

Prototype: not yet tested with real money or real doors

Lightning

  • Invoices go through BTCPay Server or LNbits.
  • The booking confirms when the invoice is paid. An unpaid hold expires and the court time opens again.
  • You choose how many sats to charge. The software does not look up a rate for you.

Cards, if you want them

  • Stripe and Square are optional. Turn on one, both, or neither.
  • The player picks Lightning or a card. Card checkout is the provider’s own page.
  • With neither card provider on, the booking page offers Lightning only.

Payments start in test mode

  • Lightning starts on a practice backend. Those invoices cannot be paid.
  • Stripe starts with a test key. Square starts in sandbox.
  • Live keys are a separate switch you choose later.

Refunds

  • Staff can refund a paid booking.
  • A player can cancel inside a window you set. You choose whether that cancel refunds.
  • On LNbits, the player provides a Lightning invoice for the refund. BTCPay, Stripe, and Square send the refund through that provider.
  • On the practice backend, a refund is recorded and no sats move.

Prices you type

  • Each court time has an amount you enter, in your currency.
  • You can also set a member price and a guest price for chosen weekdays and hours.
  • A signed-in member pays the member amount. Everyone else pays the guest amount. With no rule, the court-time amount is used.
  • There are no sample prices in the kit.

Allowance and tab

  • A membership can include an allowance. You set the amount and when it starts again: each visit, day, week, or month.
  • The allowance is used first. The rest goes on the member’s tab. The member pays the tab later with Lightning or a card you enabled.
  • A guest’s item can be charged to the host member.
  • Lines in the ledger cannot be edited or deleted. Unused allowance does not roll forward and is not paid out.

Counter sales and vending

  • The desk can charge a drink or a small item to the allowance and the tab.
  • A device can do the same after an NFC card tap or a Lightning wallet sign-in. That sign-in does not move sats.
  • A cooler or vending-machine controller is planned. It is not included. The machine’s own board would call this software.

End of day

Totals group Lightning, Stripe, Square, and refunds for the date you pick. Card totals show up after the provider confirms the payment. This is a payment total, not a tax filing. You can also download a sales file.

Planned, not available yet

Equipment rentals, lesson payments, and a controller for a locked cooler or vending machine. Ask your own tax advisor before you turn allowances on. Stored value and sales tax are local questions.
