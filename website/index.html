Features

What's in the kit today

Everything below is in the repository now. The reference app in reference/club-box runs in one Node process with SQLite. Payments and the door controller are adapters, and the mock adapters let you try the whole flow on a laptop with no Lightning node and no hardware.

Court booking

List courts and slots, hold one, pay, and the booking confirms when payment settles. Unpaid holds expire, and a slot can't be double-booked. Players can see and cancel their own bookings from an emailed one-time code, inside a window you set.

Members and profiles

Add members by membership number, with optional name, phone, email, membership type, photo and household. Revoke or restore a member, and give one member several cards and wallet keys.

Lightning payments

Invoices through BTCPay Server (Greenfield), LNbits, or a local mock. Bookings confirm only on a signed, settled webhook. Refunds go through the same backend.

Card payments: Stripe and Square

Turn on Stripe, Square, or both, and players pick Lightning or a card on the provider's hosted checkout. Test and sandbox modes are the default; live keys need an explicit switch.

Wallet door access (LNURL-auth)

Members enroll a Lightning wallet once. At the door the kiosk shows a rotating QR; the wallet signs it and an active member gets an entry pulse. No sats move to open a door. Expiry, replay and rate limits are checked, and every attempt is logged.

NFC member cards

NTAG 424 DNA (SUN) and MIFARE DESFire EV2/EV3 proofs, with enroll, revoke and lost. A card programming command is included. Clonable UID-only cards stay off unless you turn on an insecure dev switch.

Member and guest check-in

An unlock records a check-in and the front desk sees who's on site. Guests are off until you set a cap; named guests acknowledge a waiver, and marketing consent is opt-in and stored apart from the visit.

Read-only Playtomic sync

Optional import of Playtomic bookings and payments through its third-party API. Imported time blocks Lightning holds on mapped courts. It never writes back; two-way sync stays with Playtomic Connect.

Staff tools

Named staff logins with owner, manager, front-desk and coach roles, a staff audit log, a sales CSV and end-of-day totals by payment method, plus admin pages for courts, slots, members and refunds.

Encrypted backups

An online SQLite backup command, saved locally or to S3-compatible storage, with optional AES-256-GCM encryption from a passphrase you keep. Restore refuses a wrong or missing passphrase.

Self-hosted with Docker

Docker Compose runs the app with Caddy for HTTPS in front, and an optional example door relay. Your data stays on your own machine.

Playbooks, templates and manuals

Concept and phases, a pilot checklist, a Bitcoin hub playbook, tournaments, community growth, sponsorship (not investment), questions to settle with a CPA and attorney before a business holds Bitcoin, plus staff and operator manuals.

Safety by design. The app can send exactly one door command: a short entry pulse. It can't lock a door shut or control an exit. Request-to-exit, panic hardware and fire-alarm release stay on circuits this software can't reach. Design the physical door with a licensed installer and your fire marshal.

How it works

One small server, two jobs

The club box sits on a small always-on computer at the club. Players and members only need a phone. Paying is the only time money moves.

Booking a court

  1. Player picks a sloton the booking page from any phone.
  2. Club box holds itand creates a Lightning invoice or a Stripe/Square checkout link.
  3. Player paysthrough BTCPay, LNbits, Stripe or Square.
  4. Signed webhook arrivesand the hold becomes a booking. Unpaid holds expire.

Opening the door

  1. Kiosk shows a QRa fresh LNURL-auth challenge that rotates.
  2. Wallet signs itwith the member's club-only key, or the member taps an NFC card. No sats move.
  3. Club box checkssignature, expiry, replay, active member and area hours. Logs it.
  4. Relay pulses entryon a private network, and a check-in is recorded.

Outside the software: leaving the building, request-to-exit and fire-alarm release. If the server is down, members use a fob, a PIN or staff.

Roadmap

What's done and what's next

From docs/ROADMAP.md. The member-perk ideas reuse the same LNURL-auth identity (no sats move) and the NFC card fallback. Design notes are in lnurl-club-ideas.md.

Shipped Done

  • Lightning court booking (BTCPay, LNbits, mock)
  • LNURL-auth door access with audit log
  • Door hardware notes and installer checklist
  • NFC member cards (NTAG 424 DNA, DESFire)
  • Profiles and guest check-in
  • Read-only Playtomic sync
  • Staff and operator manuals

Next: member perks Next

  • Allowance and tab ledger: per-member allowances, a tab settled by Lightning or card, guest items on the host's tab
  • Member vending: scan or tap at a locked cooler or an MDB vending machine
  • Lockers: claim one for the visit and open it again
  • Booking-gated equipment: court lights, ball machine, gear cabinet
  • Member-vouched guest passes
  • Workshop slide deck, spreadsheet financial model, Spanish translation, case studies

Later / ideas Ideas

  • Signed tournament check-in and two-sided score confirmation
  • Prizes, streak and referral rewards through LNURL-withdraw
  • Coach tips through a Lightning address or LNURL-pay
  • Gift cards (LNURL-withdraw or Bolt Card)
  • Member voting, one signature per member
  • Late-cancel compensation, pay-per-minute equipment, a kiosk partner-finder board

Free allowances and sats rewards can have sales-tax and income-tax implications for the operator. Check with a tax advisor before turning them on.

Hardware

What a club needs, by category

No prices and no preferred vendors. Names are category examples, not certifications. The full list is in the installation manual and the door hardware notes.

The physical door is designed with a licensed installer and the authority having jurisdiction.
CategoryWhat the kit expects
Club computerAny small always-on machine that runs Node.js 22.13+ or Docker. One app process per SQLite file.
Kiosk screenA browser on the club network that can open /kiosk.
Desk computerA browser that can open /desk and /admin.
LightningBTCPay Server or LNbits somewhere the app can reach, or no node at all with the mock backend.
Door lockElectric strike, magnetic lock, or electrified lockset, chosen by the locksmith under code.
RelayA private-network device that accepts the pulse-entry request (Shelly-class, ESP32-class, or a Wiegand/OSDP controller as examples).
Exit and fire releaseRequired where code says so, and never connected to this software.
Lock powerA supply sized for the lock, with battery backup if code or the insurer expects it.
NFC cards and readerNTAG 424 DNA cards, and a PC/SC reader (ACR122U-class or ACR1252-class) for programming. A phone can also read the card.
Fallback entryFob, PIN or staff, so a dead server never traps anyone outside.
NetworkInternet so phones and the Lightning backend can reach the club box. The relay stays on a private network.

Get started

Try it on a laptop in minutes

The mock backends need no Lightning node and no door hardware. Commands are from the club-box README; run them from reference/club-box.

Run locally with the mock backend

git clone https://github.com/vectordesigngroup/bitpadel.git
cd bitpadel/reference/club-box
cp .env.example .env

Edit .env: set CURRENCY, CURRENCY_DECIMALS and ADMIN_TOKEN (at least 16 characters, for example openssl rand -hex 24). Leave PAYMENT_BACKEND=mock and DOOR_BACKEND=mock.

npm ci
npm run dev

Then open http://127.0.0.1:3000/ for booking, /admin, /kiosk, /desk and /guest. Requires Node.js 22.13 or newer.

Docker Compose

cp .env.example .env
# set CURRENCY, CURRENCY_DECIMALS, ADMIN_TOKEN, and PUBLIC_BASE_URL
docker compose up --build

Tests

npm test
npm run typecheck

Starting a club

  1. Read concept and phases and pick a phase (most clubs should start with one pilot court).
  2. Fill in the blank financial model with your own local quotes.
  3. Work through the pilot checklist with your host site, permitting office and insurance broker.
  4. Pick a Lightning stack from Lightning integrations and test with small payments before opening.
  5. Start the community before the court.
  6. Talk to professionals (attorney, CPA, insurance broker) before you take money from the public or hold Bitcoin as a business.
