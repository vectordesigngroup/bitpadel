Members

Use a membership number or other label. Do not put a person's name here unless your privacy policy says so.

Plan allowance

The club sets this amount. It is a recurring credit for one membership type. Spending uses the allowance first, then the tab. Unused credit from an earlier period is not paid out. Before a club uses this, a CPA and a Florida attorney should review stored value, unused balances, sales tax on items, and sats accounting.

Ledger

Each line is kept. Amounts are whole minor units. A credit reduces the tab. A charge can also record sats when the club set a sats-per-minor rate.

Download ledger CSV

Staff accounts

Owners and managers can add accounts. A manager cannot create an owner. The admin token still works.

Price rules

Weekdays are 0 Sunday through 6 Saturday, in the club timezone. A court-specific rule beats an all-courts rule. Leave court blank for every court.

Sales report

Lightning bookings and stored Playtomic payment rows in the date range. Playtomic amounts stay as imported text.

End of day

Totals by Lightning, Stripe, Square, and refunds. Card totals appear after a webhook confirms the payment.
